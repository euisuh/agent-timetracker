# Weekly purpose review

Review existing initiative records once a week. Keep evidence in the research,
application, or product repository where it already belongs; this template does
not require a new database or a note for every agent segment.

| Initiative | Intended purpose | Evidence since last review | Next decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| Research direction | Research | Link a tested claim, negative result, or unresolved method issue | Validate, revise, or stop the direction |
| Engineering contribution | Developer & career | Link review feedback, accepted work, or a design explanation you can defend | Address feedback, demonstrate understanding, or choose the next contribution |
| Product | Business & assets | Link user feedback, pilot usage, pricing response, or financial/operational result | Test the biggest commercial uncertainty |
| Shared service | Support & life | Link recovery or reliability evidence | Maintain what currently blocks useful work |

These are prompts, not recorded achievements. Ask: what changed, what remains
uncertain, and what is the smallest next action that resolves it? Do not infer
learning from delegated output, revenue from features, or completed practice from
an agent offering a lesson. Occasionally demonstrate understanding by reproducing
an analysis, defending a design tradeoff, or attempting an exercise yourself.

Use the dashboard's purpose totals to inspect recorded activity and its coverage.
Show Unclassified rather than inventing history. Concurrent agents, idle intervals,
auto-closed timers and overlapping native token counters prevent those totals
from measuring human effort, mastery, or economic return.
